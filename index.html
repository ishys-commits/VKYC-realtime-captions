CONCEPT 01

Readiness carousel — CTAs stay pinned

The version you liked — fixed so both buttons are always on screen

The three items become auto-advancing cards, PAN first and largest with the real card art. It plays on its own so users just watch (swipe is optional). Critically, “Start Video KYC” and “Schedule for later” are pinned below the carousel at all times — never a slide — so no one is ever unsure what to do.

CONCEPT 02

Original vs. copy

A ~1s animation above the real PAN card — no taps

Only the PAN zone animates. Three decoy thumbnails — photocopy, screenshot, digital image — get an ✗, grey out and fall away, while the real PAN card lights up with an indigo ring and the caption briefly reads “Original only — not a copy.” It teaches the #1 failure by showing, not warning. Everything else is untouched.

CONCEPT 04

Sequential focus

Same list — a moving spotlight highlights each item in turn

The exact current layout, but a gentle spotlight walks down the list once: the real PAN card lifts and outlines first (held longest) with a one-line helper — “Original card only — no copy or screenshot” — then pen & paper, then internet. It guides the eye through everything in ~6 seconds with no taps, then rests. Both CTAs stay put.
