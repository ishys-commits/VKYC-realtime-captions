Copy alternatives & recommendation

  • A · Payment Timed Out — “We didn’t receive your UPI payment within the time limit. Don’t worry — no money was deducted.” Safest, most literal fit for the brief.
  • B · Let’s try that again — “We waited, but your UPI app didn’t confirm in time.” Warmest; good if the wider flow already uses a friendly voice.
  • C · Session Expired — “Your 10-minute payment window has closed.” Matches banking/UPI conventions; the status pill reinforces that no debit happened.
  • D · Almost there — momentum-first; best when drop-off after timeout is a real concern.
  • Recommendation: ship A as the default (clearest, least ambiguous) and reuse the “no money was deducted” reassurance line wherever a variant is chosen — it’s the single most important thing to tell an anxious user at a payment timeout.
