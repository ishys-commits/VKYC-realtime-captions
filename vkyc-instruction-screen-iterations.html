Video KYC — instruction screen refinements

Three iterations of the same screen. Same colours, fonts, card look and corner styles as the original — this is a content & hierarchy refinement, not a redesign. Built for budget Android, 360×640, tier 2/3 users: low reading, high recognition.

Scroll the phones — the dashed red line marks the 640 px fold. In all three, “Start Video KYC” sits above it.

All 3 share: permissions removed · “can’t get up” banner · 2 mandatory items with weight · plain language · unticked consent · language toggle · Listen · Schedule + “Ready in 5” buttons.
